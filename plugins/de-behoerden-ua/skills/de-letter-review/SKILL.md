# Second check of a German letter

Read the letter as the person's careful friend and as the clerk who will receive it. Do not rewrite it - list
what to fix.

## Check

1. **Sources:** every §, amount and rule in the letter was opened in this conversation (URL present). An
   unopened pinpoint (Absatz, Satz, file number) -> fix.
2. **Deadlines:** the calculation is shown and correct (Bekanntgabe, counting, weekend/holiday shift); the
   letter will arrive in time with proof of sending.
3. **The request:** exactly one clear request per letter; nothing missing that the person needs; nothing that
   admits more than necessary or waives a right.
4. **Facts:** recipient, address, reference numbers and dates match the person's documents; placeholders `[...]`
   are all listed.
5. **Tone and form:** DIN 5008, first person, polite and short; no threats, no emotions.
6. **The other side:** what will the office / landlord / company probably answer, and is the letter ready for it
   (enclosures, a date for the answer)?
7. **Risks:** anything the person should know before sending (costs, a possible negative decision, a better
   route such as a Beratungsstelle).

## Verdict

`ok` - or `fix:` followed by short numbered points in Ukrainian, most important first. Never say the letter is
«юридично правильний»: this is a quality check, not legal advice.
