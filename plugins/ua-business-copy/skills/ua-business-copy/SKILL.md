# Ukrainian business copy

Write for a busy owner and their customers: short, concrete, honest. The reader decides in seconds whether the
text is about them.

## 1. Before writing - get the facts

Ask (or find in the brief) only what changes the text:
- who the business is, the city, what exactly they sell, for whom, the price range if they publish it;
- what makes them different, in their own words (not «якісно та недорого»);
- the one action the reader should take (call, book, write in Telegram, buy).

Missing fact -> write `[уточнити: ...]` in the text and list it at the end. Never fill a gap with a plausible
invention.

## 2. Pick the format

| Format | Shape |
|---|---|
| Site page (home, service, about) | headline = the benefit in 4-8 words; one-line subhead; 3-5 short blocks, each a heading + 2-4 sentences; one call to action repeated at the end |
| SEO article | one target search phrase per article (never one another article of the same site already targets); title <= 60 chars, meta description <= 155; H1, H2/H3 that answer real questions; 1200-1800 words; a short FAQ; 2+ links to real pages of the site |
| Beginner explainer («що таке ... простими словами») | 900-1500 words; every term explained the first time; an everyday example in every section (a coffee shop, a hairdresser, a family budget); one soft link to the business at the end |
| Social post | the hook in the first line; one idea; 3-6 short lines; one question to the reader at the end; hashtags only if the platform needs them |

## 3. Language: plain modern Ukrainian

Run `checklist.md` on every text. The biggest offenders:
- surzhyk and calques: «приймати участь» -> «брати участь», «на протязі» -> «протягом», «являється» -> «є»,
  «слідуючий» -> «наступний», «в залежності від» -> «залежно від», «співпадати» -> «збігатися»;
- bureaucratic chains: «здійснюємо надання послуг» -> «надаємо послуги» -> better «робимо»;
- empty praise: «високоякісний», «індивідуальний підхід», «команда професіоналів» - replace with a fact
  («відповідаємо за 15 хвилин», «12 років у професії»);
- address the reader as «ви» consistently; short sentences (<= 20 words) beat long ones.

## 4. Facts and numbers - every one needs a source

Keep a `claims` list next to the text:

```
claim: "доставка за 40 хвилин по центру"   source: the owner's brief (message of 2026-03-02)
claim: "70% покупців шукають відгуки"       source: https://... (opened, quote the line)
```

- A number with no source is removed, not softened.
- Never write fake reviews, clients, results, awards, «топ-1 у місті» or guarantees of search positions.
- Prices only from the business's own price list.

## 5. Hand-over

Give the text, then: the `claims` list, the `[уточнити]` list, and for SEO the target phrase plus 10-20 related
phrases you used. Say plainly what you could not check.
