# German deadlines (Fristen)

Never compute silently: show every step so the person can check it. Open each rule (skill `de-legal-research`)
before relying on it.

## Rules to open

- **Bekanntgabe by post:** a Bescheid counts as received on the 4th day after posting (§ 37 Abs. 2 SGB X;
  for the Finanzamt § 122a / § 122 Abs. 2 AO - check the current wording). If it actually arrived later, the real
  date counts - ask.
- **Counting:** the day of the event does not count (§ 187 Abs. 1 BGB); a month period ends on the same-numbered
  day of the next month (§ 188 Abs. 2 BGB); an end on Saturday, Sunday or a public holiday moves to the next
  working day (§ 193 BGB; § 26 Abs. 3 SGB X; § 64 Abs. 3 SGG). Public holidays differ by Land.
- **Widerspruch** against a Jobcenter or Sozialamt Bescheid: 1 month (§ 84 SGG). **Einspruch** against a tax
  Bescheid: 1 month (§ 355 AO).
- **No or a wrong Rechtsbehelfsbelehrung:** usually 1 year instead of 1 month (§ 66 Abs. 2 SGG; § 58 Abs. 2 VwGO;
  § 356 Abs. 2 AO).
- **Rent increase to the local comparison rent:** consent until the end of the 2nd full month after receipt
  (§ 558b BGB).
- **Court Mahnbescheid:** Widerspruch within 2 weeks (§ 692 ZPO) - always urgent, suggest professional help.

## How to write a deadline

One line with the calculation:

> Бешайд датований 03.11 → вважається отриманим 06.11 → заперечення (Widerspruch) до 08.12 (понеділок, бо
> 06.12 - субота).

- Unknown date (posting or receipt) -> «[ПЕРЕВІРИТИ]», ask, and give no guessed date.
- Plausibility: a Widerspruch period of 3 days or 6 months means you misread something - re-read.
- Advise sending a few days early and with proof: Einschreiben, fax with a transmission report, an upload
  receipt from the office's portal, or a stamped copy when handing it in.
